# Code of conduct

We want Gami.Fied to be a project anyone can take part in, whatever their experience, background or identity. This code applies in the repository, its issues, discussions and pull requests, and anywhere else someone represents the project.

It is based on the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Respect different views and experience, and disagree about ideas, not people.
- Give and accept feedback on the work graciously.
- Own your mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from them.
- Think about what is best for the people who use and build the project.

## Not accepted

- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Insults, put-downs, trolling, and attacks on someone's identity or beliefs.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as an address, without their permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainers by email at **gami.conduct@fied.cc**. Every report is reviewed, and the reporter's privacy is respected.

## What maintainers may do

Maintainers decide what to do about behaviour that breaks this code. Depending on how serious it is and whether it is repeated, that can be:

1. **A correction:** a private, written warning that explains what was wrong. A public apology may be asked for.
2. **A warning:** a final warning, with limits on contact with the people involved for a set time.
3. **A temporary ban:** no taking part in the project for a set time.
4. **A permanent ban:** for a pattern of breaking the code, harassment, or aggression towards a group.

Maintainers may remove or edit comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code.
